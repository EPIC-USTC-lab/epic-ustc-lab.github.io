abstract: "In recent years, the extensive application of Deep Neural Networks (DNNs) in industrial IOT (IIoT) has driven the development of smart factories. To meet the real-time processing demands of diverse tasks, collaborative computing across multiple devices has become a critical solution. In this case, the heterogeneity of edge devices necessitates performance-aware task scheduling. However, existing performance-aware algorithms face significant overhead challenges in large-scale edge environments. Therefore, this paper proposes HeterScale, a hierarchical scheduling framework that reduces global scheduling overhead through device grouping and intra-group performance-aware scheduling, while improving the task completion ratio (TCR) under deadline constraints. First, we introduce a data-driven device feature extraction method to support performance prediction. Second, a Transformer-based model is employed to predict the performance boundaries of device groups, and constraint programming (CP) is used to generate device groupings. Third, a performance-aware scheduling algorithm enables efficient task scheduling within each device group. Extensive experimental results demonstrate that HeterScale outperforms state-of-the-art methods in large-scale heterogeneous scheduling scenarios, reducing scheduling overhead by up to 29.51% while achieving significant improvements in TCR."
